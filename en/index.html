What the app does

Three checks, one answer: you know what is holding the post back before you publish.

Score a video

Pick a video from your library. On Android you can also share it straight out of TikTok into Mr. Viral. You get a score plus a breakdown by hook, pacing, visuals and sound.

Test your opening line

The first 3 seconds decide everything. Paste your hook and see how it lands, with suggestions that pull harder.

Analyse your script

Good pacing keeps viewers to the end. Paste your script and see where retention breaks and whether your call to action works.

More than a score

Mr. Viral rewrites your hook and your script on request. You see the AI version next to your own and decide which one you keep.

No account, no email

No login, no sign-up. On first launch your device gets an anonymous ID and you can start right away. Your videos, hooks and scripts stay yours. We do not use them for advertising and we do not train AI models on them. What exactly gets processed is set out in the privacy policy.
