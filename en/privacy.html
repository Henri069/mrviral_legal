Privacy Policy

Mr. Viral · Last updated: 13 September 2026

In short
  • No account, no email address, no password. Just a random, anonymous ID for your device.
  • Your video is processed for exactly one purpose: the analysis. Right afterwards it is deleted — both by us and at Google.
  • What stays behind is the result: the score, the breakdown and a small preview image.
  • Ads only in the free version and only after your consent; anonymous usage statistics you can switch off in the app at any time. No selling of data.
  • Deletion works by email. The Delete data page explains how.

2. An anonymous ID instead of a user account

Mr. Viral has no sign-up. On first launch the app signs in anonymously with our backend (Supabase, anonymous sign-in). This creates a random ID that is stored on your device. That ID serves one purpose only: linking your analyses to your device so that nobody else can reach them.

We never learn your name, your email address or your phone number. The ID is not linked to any other service. There is no login and no password recovery.

Important: because there is no account, everything hangs on that ID. If you delete the app, sign out inside the app or switch to a new device, the old ID is gone — and with it your access to your history. The data itself is still with us, but nobody can retrieve it any more. If you want it erased there too, write to us beforehand (see Delete data).

Hiding is not deleting

When you swipe an analysis away in your history, it disappears from the app but the row stays on the server. The reason: your monthly allowance is metered by counting your analyses. If swiping deleted the row, the allowance would be unlimited. The app points this out when you confirm. Permanent erasure happens on request — the Delete data page explains how.

4. Where your video actually goes

So you can follow the path your material takes:

  1. You share a video from TikTok into Mr. Viral, or pick one on your device.
  2. The app shrinks it on your device and uploads it into our private storage (Supabase, Ireland region). The folder belongs to your ID alone.
  3. Our server function fetches the file from there and uploads it to Google's Gemini interface for analysis.
  4. Google analyses the video and returns a result in text form.
  5. The video is then deleted immediately — at Google and in our storage.
  6. What remains: the score, the breakdown, the AI's text and a preview image.

Videos may be at most 90 seconds long and 50 MB in size. If an analysis fails before it ever reaches the server, the app clears the uploaded file itself.

5. Analysis by Google Gemini

The actual scoring is done by an AI model from Google (Gemini API). What goes there:

  • for a video analysis: the video itself,
  • for a hook or script analysis: the text you typed in,
  • plus our instruction on how it should be scored.

No ID, no name and no other identifier of yours goes with it. Google only receives the content and does not know who it came from.

Google runs this interface on its own infrastructure, including outside the EU. An uploaded video file is deleted at Google by us right after the analysis. For paid use of the Gemini API, Google undertakes not to use the submitted content to train its models. Mr. Viral uses the interface on a paid basis.

Please do not upload or type in anything you would not show to an external AI service. Details on what Google does with the data are in Google's own terms for the Gemini API.

6. Access to your videos and photos

To pick a video, Mr. Viral opens your operating system's file picker. The app gets no access to your whole gallery, only to the file you tap yourself. We do not scan your gallery and upload nothing you did not select.

If you share a video straight from TikTok, your operating system hands exactly that one file to Mr. Viral. We have no access to your TikTok account, no connection to TikTok, and see neither your followers nor your statistics.

7. Advertising, consent and usage statistics

Ads in the free version

Without a subscription you unlock an analysis by watching a short ad video. The ads are served by Google AdMob. Before the first ad loads, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform, following the IAB's TCF standard) in the EU, the EEA and the United Kingdom. There you decide whether ads may be personalised and whether information may be stored on your device. If you decline, you get non-personalised ads — the app works exactly the same. On iOS the system's App Tracking Transparency prompt follows; if you declined in the form, it is not shown at all. You can change your choice at any time under Profile → Ad settings. With Mr. Viral Pro there are no ads.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see which features are used and where the app stalls or crashes, we use PostHog (PostHog Inc., run on servers in Frankfurt, EU). Transmitted are your anonymous ID, device model, operating system, app version, language, which screens you open and which actions you take — for example “analysis started”, the score band of a result, or whether the paywall was opened — plus error and crash reports. Never transmitted are the content of your videos, hooks or scripts, and no file names. The IP address is discarded on receipt. We use the same data to compare design variants of the app (A/B tests); your anonymous ID is assigned to a variant for that.

The statistics run only if you agreed in the consent form to information being stored on your device (legal basis Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TTDSG); outside the scope of the GDPR, where no form appears, they run without one. You can switch them off at any time under Profile → Legal → “Anonymous usage statistics”. Retention: 12 months. There is no profiling, no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR and no selling of data.

The app's typeface ships inside the app and is not fetched over the internet.

9. Security

  • All connections between app and server are encrypted with TLS.
  • The storage is private. Access happens only through short-lived, signed links.
  • The database enforces the separation itself: every query only sees rows belonging to your own ID (Row Level Security).
  • On every access the server derives who the data belongs to from the verified sign-in token — never from a value sent by the device.
  • The key for the AI interface lives on the server only, never in the app.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data (Art. 15)
  • Rectification (Art. 16)
  • Erasure (Art. 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)

You exercise these rights by email to henri.irmscher@gmail.com. We respond within one month.

Important: because Mr. Viral does not know who you are, we can only handle an email request if you include your anonymous ID. Where to find it is explained on the Delete data page. Without that ID we cannot locate your data and, for security reasons, cannot hand anything over (Art. 11(2) GDPR).

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Ours is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Postfach 3163, 65021 Wiesbaden, Germany.
