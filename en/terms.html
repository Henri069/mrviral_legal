2. What ViraKin does

You submit a video, a hook line or a script. ViraKin sends it to an AI model and gives you a rating back: a score from 0 to 100, a breakdown by category and concrete suggestions for improvement. The app itself computes nothing; it forwards to our service and displays the result.

3. The score is an assessment, not a prediction

This is the most important point in these terms. The score is the opinion of an AI model that rates against known patterns for short-form video. It is not a promise and not a prediction of how your video will actually perform.

  • We have no access to TikTok's algorithm and no connection to TikTok.
  • A high score guarantees no views, no followers and no reach.
  • A low score does not mean your video is bad.
  • Two analyses of the same video can produce slightly different results. That is how AI models work.
  • AI models get things wrong. The suggestions are prompts for thought, not instructions.

Decisions about your content, your business or your ad spend are yours. We are not liable for the success or failure of your videos.
